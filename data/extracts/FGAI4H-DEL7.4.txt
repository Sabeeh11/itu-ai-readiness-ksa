FOUR EVALUATION PHASES:
Phase 1 - Model design and purpose. Definition of intended use, target population, clinical question and success criteria.
Phase 2 - Algorithmic validation. Evaluation on development data and on external test data drawn from a different source than the training data.
Phase 3 - Clinical validation. Interventional or clinical studies evaluating the model in the intended clinical setting.
Phase 4 - Deployment and ongoing monitoring, including periodic algorithmic audits.

CROSS-CUTTING. Ethical considerations and economic evaluation apply across all four phases. The deliverable carries an explicit focus on equity in low and middle income countries.

RELATIONSHIP TO NATIONAL REGIMES. The deliverable provides an internationally agreed structure for clinical evaluation of health AI. In the Saudi context there is no national counterpart: IS0304 provides conformance certification for interoperability only, and no Saudi body validates or benchmarks model performance. This absence is recorded as gap G5.
