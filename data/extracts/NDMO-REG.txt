CURATED EXTRACT - National Data Governance Interim Regulations, Version 1, 1 June 2020
Issuer: National Data Management Office (NDMO), under SDAIA
Source (Ministry of Finance mirror): https://www.mof.gov.sa/en/generalservcies/open-data/Documents/PoliciesEn.pdf
NOTE: Curated summary of verified provisions, not the official text.

STRUCTURE. Contains five sub-regulations: Data Classification, Personal Data Protection, Data Sharing, Freedom of Information, and Open Data.

DATA CLASSIFICATION. Four levels: Top Secret, Secret, Confidential (with sub-categories A, B and C), and Public.

DATA LOCALISATION. Personal data shall be stored and processed within the Kingdom's territory. Offshore processing requires written regulatory approval coordinated with NDMO.

DATA SHARING. Governs the conditions under which government entities may share data with each other and with third parties, including the classification-dependent controls that apply.
