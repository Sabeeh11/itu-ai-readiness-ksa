PROGRAMME. Ehalati is the national electronic medical referral programme, operated through the Saudi Medical Appointments and Referrals Centre (SMARC). Launched 2012; SMARC established 2019; unified platform inaugurated 19 May 2024. Hotline 937.

REFERRAL URGENCY CATEGORIES AND TIME STANDARDS (as published by MOH):
- Life-saving: immediate, via the 24 hour hotline.
- Emergency: maximum 24 hours.
- Routine inpatient: maximum 3 days.
- Routine outpatient: maximum 7 days.

DISCREPANCY WARNING. Peer-reviewed literature (Healthcare 2025;13(16):1945) reports different standards for the same categories: emergency maximum 72 hours, routine inpatient maximum 2 weeks, routine outpatient maximum 4 weeks. Any citation of a service level standard must acknowledge this discrepancy.

CARE HIERARCHY. Referrals move through a four-level hierarchy of care.

CONTENT STANDARD - VERIFIED ABSENCE. The programme publishes time standards governing how quickly a referral must be responded to. No published national standard defines what content a referral document must contain in order to be complete. This absence is recorded as gap G11 and is directly material to any system that scores referral completeness.
