CURATED EXTRACT - Guideline for Personal Data Destruction, Anonymization and Pseudonymization
Arabic title: الدليل الاسترشادي لإتلاف البيانات الشخصية وإخفاء الهوية والترميز
Issuer: SDAIA. Published in Arabic only; no English edition located.
Source: https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PersonalDataDestruction
NOTE: Curated summary of verified content, not the official text.

TECHNIQUES NAMED FOR ANONYMISATION:
- Generalization: replacing a precise value with a broader category, for example replacing an exact age with an age band.
- Aggregation: replacing an individual value with a grouped value, for example replacing a full date of birth with a birth year.
- Masking: obscuring part or all of an attribute value.

TECHNIQUES NAMED FOR PSEUDONYMISATION:
- Strong encryption with encryption keys stored separately from the encrypted data.
- Coding: replacing an identifying value such as a name with a reference number, with the mapping held separately.

IMPACT ASSESSMENT. The guideline requires an assessment covering the possibility of re-identification.

WHAT THE GUIDELINE DOES NOT CONTAIN (verified by inspection):
- No k-anonymity requirement or parameter.
- No l-diversity or t-closeness requirement.
- No hashing algorithm specification.
- No quantitative re-identification risk methodology.
- No enumerated schedule of identifiers requiring removal.
- No acceptable residual risk threshold.

This absence is recorded as gap G4. The comparative benchmark is the US HHS HIPAA de-identification guidance, which specifies both an Expert Determination pathway with a "very small risk" standard and a Safe Harbor pathway enumerating eighteen specific identifiers.
