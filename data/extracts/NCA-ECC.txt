CURATED EXTRACT - Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC-2:2024), Version 2
Issuer: National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA). Supersedes ECC-1:2018.
Source: https://cdn.nca.gov.sa/api/files/public/upload/86e09090-44e4-481f-bc28-355673607654_ECC--2024-EN.pdf
NOTE: Curated summary of verified findings, not the official text.

STRUCTURE. Four domains - Cybersecurity Governance, Cybersecurity Defense, Cybersecurity Resilience, and Third-Party and Cloud Computing Cybersecurity. 28 subdomains. 108 main controls and 92 subcontrols.

APPLICABILITY. Government entities and their affiliates, and private sector operators of Critical National Infrastructure.

DATA LOCALISATION. Data localisation mandates present in ECC-1:2018 were removed from ECC-2:2024 and transferred to the National Data Management Office. Entities must consult NDMO regarding permissible data location.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE - VERIFIED ABSENCE. All four domains and all 108 main controls were inspected. ECC-2:2024 contains no controls, subcontrols, or provisions addressing artificial intelligence or machine learning systems. An AI system processing health data within a Saudi entity inherits generic cloud, data and application controls with no AI-specific cybersecurity requirement. This absence is recorded as gap G10.
