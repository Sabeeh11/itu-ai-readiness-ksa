CURATED EXTRACT - Telehealth Application Guidelines
Issuer: National Health Information Center (NHIC) / Saudi Health Council
CURRENCY WARNING: No version number or publication date is printed in the document.
Source: https://nhic.gov.sa/standards/Telehealth/Telehealth-Application-Guidelines.pdf
NOTE: Curated summary of verified provisions, not the official text.

INFRASTRUCTURE RELIABILITY. Equipment must be reliable and must work well over the locally available network and bandwidth. Tier 3 to Tier 4 infrastructure is recommended for critical cases. Disaster recovery specifications apply. Display calibration requirements apply to diagnostic imaging modalities. Interoperability across modalities is required.

DATA RESIDENCY. Data storage should be hosted within Saudi Arabia's geographical boundaries. Cloud computing compliance obligations apply.

SECURITY. Encryption protocols are required. Role-based access control is required. Compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law is required.

SCOPE. Covers eight categories of telehealth service, together with cross-border provisions, and addresses emerging technologies including artificial intelligence and blockchain.
