Issuer: ITU-T / WHO Focus Group on Artificial Intelligence for Health
Source: https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-t/opb/fg/T-FG-AI4H-2023-8-PDF-E.pdf
NOTE: Curated summary of verified content, not the official text.

DATA LIFECYCLE. Governance of create, read, update and delete operations across the data lifecycle.
SECURITY. Encryption in transit and at rest. Secure data centre hosting. Authentication and role-based access control.
TEST DATA. Undisclosed test data must remain undisclosed, so that model evaluation is not contaminated. This aligns with the SFDA requirement that clinical test reference datasets not be publicly available.
OWNERSHIP. Data ownership licence agreements are required between parties.
INTEROPERABILITY. Four layers of interoperability are described.
AUDIT. Comprehensive logging of access and processing events is required.

LIMITATION. The deliverable references compliance with GDPR and HIPAA but does not itself detail de-identification techniques or thresholds.
